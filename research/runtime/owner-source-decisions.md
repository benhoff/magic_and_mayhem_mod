## Recovered decision branch

`423590` selects either the normal task/merge path or split-first search when it
resets. Normal search does not fall through into partition search after its merge
candidates are exhausted. The reset split-first predicates include an unparented
source with at least two available members, no larger available same-owner root,
no healthy task, nonpositive summed net regional danger, fewer than20 same-owner
roots and the two specialized queue counts below3. The native bounded domain has
exactly two ordinary friendlies and at most two terminal roots; the native queue
folding and active-health availability producers remain explicit host policies.

The pinned merge and partition proposal caps at `5c5340`/`5c5344` are both1.
For two friendlies there is one nontrivial partition and at most one other root.
The first partition selects the faster creature; equal speed selects the first
member in the compared traversal. The complement receives no independently
scored task in this branch and is registered for later planning. The half-source
score is compared with the shared best score, not added to a second half score.

Normal stage0 selects from the current source. Stage1 collects the sole available
other root and consumes a strategic RNG draw for its partial shuffle, even when
there is only one candidate. Stage2 evaluates the combined source with bias
`-(other_task.value+1)`, or zero when the other source has no task. It applies the
other task's replacement filter before bias and rejects exact candidate1.
Maximum/weighted selection and shared ties retain the existing4284e0 contract.
Temporary synthetic source roots also incur the selector's root budget debit.
Budget exhaustion retains the current phase and evaluator state. After selection,
commit removes the winning bias before the current-source replacement predicate.
The existing NS84 selected topology helpers apply displaced retirement and source
rebinding; the original installation/clone/training boundaries remain separate.

The isolated decision fixture enters actual423590 at reset, calls actual4284e0,
uses membership-dependent finite evaluator scores, and observes original commit
consequences through explicit installation/registration/storage recorders.
It covers normal and split-first reset, both speed orders/equal speeds,
maximum/weighted modes, absent/completed other tasks with varied values/bias,
sliced budgets, winning member order and the complete250-word strategic RNG.
The523240 reset net-danger input is a supplied zero service; one-candidate
distance is supplied. Healthy-other-task progress/retention and larger candidate
sets remain outside this comparison.

## Per-member task6 production

Whole431ce0 traverses each friendly member and produces an independent regional
probe. A failed member probe rejects that enemy-group candidate. Successful
probes contribute maximum travel time, summed auxiliary path quantity, summed
destination danger and maximum all-path risk. The latter two sums are divided by
member count for the candidate suffix. Candidate lifetime consumes one draw after
all member probes: `truncate(exponential(mean100)+maximum_travel+600)`.

The native evaluator retains the member index, current regional search and these
aggregates across slices; members may occupy different assigned regions. Source
features use all members and installed Brain.dat inference uses the resulting72
ordered features. Source membership/task identity changes restart the native
pending search. Movement of the current probe origin restarts that probe; these
host epoch policies are not an original mutable-world equivalence claim.

The isolated431ce0 comparison starts with a prepared enemy group and two ordinary
physical friendly actors in independently assigned regions. It executes actual
ordered traversal, regional search, path accumulation, feature construction and
lifetime generation. Finite regional graphs, empty resources/inventories, region
parameter1, allocator and constant inference outputs are supplied. It compares
per-slice results/budgets/full RNG and successful72 features, path totals/risk and
lifetime. Perception/group preparation and learned inference are separate existing
contracts. Unused ancillary431ce0 scratch fields are not compared ABI state.
