## Receipt information the pilot must supply

The following are researched requirements for implementation, not a fabricated
production receipt schema or a passing profile. Final collector-specific field
names and validation must be exercised against the real EP03 acquisition.

| Information | Required binding and check |
| --- | --- |
| Definition | Exact contract/gap/plan revisions and hashes; receipt schema/profile and validator/collector source identities |
| Claim | Exact requirement, configuration, finite input/domain, caller and reference policy; reject unknown/duplicate identities |
| Build and environment | Original executable, assets/saves/generated World, as-built compiler and binary closure, actual provider/display/media selections and bounded resource/progress profile |
| Acquisition | Independently acquired original/reference and native run identities; native ownership before the first required frame; disjoint owned-input/oracle roles |

| Actions and effects | Request/acknowledgement/effect identity, typed return/state values and required pixel comparisons; no equality from sampled copies or unknown acknowledgements |
| Outcome | Healthy normal Quit/owner retirement or a separately declared expected fault/restart/continuation outcome, with exact failure and fresh-generation semantics |
| Provenance and persistence | Verified payload references, current scope/scenario/source closure, immutable ledger events, complete local SQLite inputs and a verified recoverable backup |

A digest protects exact bytes; recomputing a digest over an authored dictionary
does not prove that a trusted collector observed it. The new admission path must
verify the producer, independent observations and semantic checks before it
creates an admitted execution event. There should be no generic set-pass command.

## Join and decision rules

Each of the 156 rows needs its own configuration/source/domain/caller/reference
join, with adequate evidence reused only where that relationship is established.
The machine-readable result retains every leaf's concrete missing proof and all
ten gates' required observations. Both 640x480 and 800x600 rebuild/rebind remain
required. Real next-map progression and separate legitimate victory/defeat remain
distinct from Save/Load, resize and Quit-created defeat.

Keep three outcomes separate: an admitted current in-scope mismatch, inadequate
or missing evidence, and a historical negative observation. A required ordinary
path's safe refusal or missing implementation cannot become a success. A matched
expected fault may contribute to its fault/cleanup requirement while the healthy
journey remains pending. Force-stop cannot satisfy normal Quit. A retained old
mismatch does not automatically fail an unrelated current claim.

Do not use latest-timestamp-wins. A repair needs a new receipt with an explicit
affected source/input/scope relationship, rerun proof and named superseded
failure. Retain both records. Shared capture/history changes invalidate their
consumers and return to the pilot. Cross-configuration reuse needs an explicit
per-requirement argument about provider-sensitive dependencies; one software
probe does not duplicate 78 passing outcomes.

`gate.separate-decisions` requires independently recorded verdicts, not two
positive verdicts. Supported-player acceptance may coexist with pending original
recovery under the exact admitted supplier boundary. The two decisions cannot
be coupled through a circular prerequisite or a majority/percentage calculation.
FPS remains diagnostic; response, progress and resource bounds still apply.

Original recovery consumes all 15 contract-group recovery exits, not the player
row count or a tally of implemented behaviors. The reviewed group links contain
62 recovered-behavior and 44 native-policy entries. They retain exact original
anchors, scope/confidence, evidence, independent statuses and remaining gaps.
Native policy implementation does not recover original behavior. A function
anchor does not recover every branch/caller, and permission to retain an original
supplier does not recover its internals. Installing a native replacement is also
not automatically a prerequisite for understanding the original.

The full group-by-group map is in `original_recovery_groups` in the research
result. It preserves the required surface/alias, history, World/terrain/producer,
palette/visibility/HUD, screen/alternate ownership, input/movie, storage and
teardown/admission investigations. Broader original recovery remains separate
from the first public-player pilot. The existing bounded platform answer does
not close original physical-driver behavior.
