command-session provider already contains wrapped target-direction and camera/
reverse-facing arithmetic. The entry now links that provider and bounded
synthetic controls, and moves from `none` to `partial`. The whole original
targeting/attachment/configured-action-base/lifetime contract remains unresolved.

retain their own scopes. No original comparison or live integration is granted.

entries marked none. The four remaining none entries are three unresolved
